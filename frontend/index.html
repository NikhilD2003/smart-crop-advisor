    HOW IT WORKS

    1. 01

      Sense

      Seven readings: N, P, K, pH, temperature, humidity, rainfall. Impossible values are rejected.

    2. 02

      Learn

      Decision tree, random forest and a neural network trained on 2,200 labelled farms, plus a suitability model trained on 2,200 generated weak-field rows.

    3. 03

      Reason

      A rule-based expert system chains facts into practical advice: irrigate, fertilise, correct the soil.

    4. 04

      Explain

      Every answer shows how much each reading costs the suitability score, and whether the classifiers agree.

    THE ADVISOR AS AN AGENT (PEAS)

    1. PERFORMANCE

      Accuracy and usefulness

      Crop accuracy, yield error (R², RMSE) and whether the advice helps. Not just a high score.

    2. ENVIRONMENT

      A farm

      Soil, weather and season. Partially observable (7 readings), stochastic, dynamic, mostly episodic.

    3. ACTUATORS

      Advice on screen

      Crop, suitability, yield and fixes. On a real farm this could drive irrigation or fertiliser dosing.

    4. SENSORS

      Seven readings

      N, P, K, pH, temperature, humidity, rainfall.

    Three agent views: the rule engine is a simple reflex agent (condition-action rules), the models make it a learning agent, and picking the crop with the highest suitability makes it utility-based. Future work: learn an irrigation policy from rewards (reinforcement learning).

    RESPONSIBLE USE

    1. SECURITY

      Guard the inputs

      Out-of-range or spoofed sensor values are rejected before inference. Flipping 20% of training labels cut tree accuracy from 97.7% to 81.1%, so training data must come from trusted sources.

    2. ETHICS

      Human in the loop

      The suitability score is always shown and output is advice, not a command. The data may not represent your region, and wrong advice costs real income.

    3. SOCIAL GOOD

      Less waste, more access

      Better crop fit means less water and fertiliser wasted, and expert-style guidance for small farmers who cannot afford an agronomist.

    4. PRIVACY & EDGE

      Your data stays yours

      The whole model is under 200 KB and runs in your browser, offline. Nothing you enter is sent to a server. Local-language support is future work.

    This is advice, not a command. The models learned from a public dataset of 2,200 samples and may not match your region or soil. They do not see soil type, pests or market prices. Check with a local agronomist before acting.

    Suitability scores come from a synthetic dataset the team generated (public data has no weak fields). Treat them as a guide, not measured field results.
